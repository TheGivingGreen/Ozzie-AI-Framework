# Agent Task

Use the fields relevant to the work. For low-risk advisory work, the goal and material assumptions may be captured in the response without creating a separate record. Production and external actions need the relevant scope, authority and evidence below.

## Identity and Owner

Project and task ID, requesting person, accountable owner, status and due date, where applicable.

## Goal

What should the agent accomplish?

## Task Route

Discovery, spec, plan, execute, review, validate, Git, handoff, or mixed.

## Context

What does the agent need to know?

Record applicable framework, project profile and specialist versions; authorized source IDs or references and their versions; known assumptions, conflicts and exclusions. Identify any approved master and locked elements.

## Spec Summary

What intent, requirements, and acceptance criteria have already been approved?

## Scope

What files, systems, or deliverables are in scope?

## Out Of Scope

What should the agent avoid?

## Access and Approval

Identify the execution identity, permitted sources, tools and output destinations, prohibited actions, and the owner and evidence of required controls. A brief does not grant access. Stop or narrow the task if a necessary control cannot be verified.

Identify the reviewer and decision owner, the approval required for the deliverable, and any separate approval for an external action. Record the approver, exact artifact or version, action and conditions when approved. Include budget and dependencies when relevant.
